Questions before you call

Where is the office?

11711 Bedford Street, Unit 1, Houston, Texas 77031, in southwest Houston. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Any letter or notice you have received, with every page and the envelope. Identification. Any papers already filed in your matter. Use the "prepare your call" list above for your type of case.

Can I bring someone to translate?

Yes. Bring a family member or an interpreter you trust. We take the time needed to make sure you understand each step.

How do I know the firm is legitimate?

Look up the attorney on the State Bar of Texas website. Every licensed Texas lawyer appears there with their license status. Ask any lawyer for their bar number and check it.

Is my information confidential?

What you tell an attorney in a consultation is protected. Please keep sensitive details out of the website form until we have spoken and confirmed representation in writing.
