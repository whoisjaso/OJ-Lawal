The attorney you actually speak with

Omotayo J. Lawal, known to clients as O. J., founded the firm to practice law the way a neighbor would want it practiced: directly, carefully and without pretence.

Clients deal with the attorney, not a call center. Papers are read by the person who will sign them. Questions are answered in plain words, in person or on the phone, and in the client's own language wherever a family member or interpreter can help.

How the firm works

  • Honest first assessment

    If a path exists, you hear what it is and what it costs. If it does not exist yet, you hear that too. Nobody is sold a case.

  • The attorney prepares the file

    Forms and filings are prepared and reviewed by the attorney, checked against your history for consistency.

  • Deadlines come first

    Any date on any paper you bring is calendared before the first meeting ends.

  • You are told what happened

    After every filing, hearing or call with the other side, you learn what it means and what comes next.

  • Written fee agreements

    What the fee covers is in writing before work begins, and every payment is receipted.
