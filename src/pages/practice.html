What we handle

Each area below lists the kinds of matters clients bring, and the worry that usually comes with them.

  1. Family law

    I want this to end fairly, and I am scared of what it does to the children.

    Divorce, child custody and visitation, child support, property division, modifications and enforcement of existing orders. The aim is a settlement where one is possible and a prepared case where it is not. Where a spouse's immigration status is tied to the marriage, that is addressed before anything is filed.

  2. Criminal defense

    One charge, and I could lose my job, my record and maybe my status.

    Misdemeanor and felony charges in Harris County and surrounding courts, from the first appearance through trial or negotiated resolution. For non-citizens, every plea is reviewed for its immigration consequences first, because a sentence that looks small can carry a very large second penalty.

  3. Business and commercial law

    I built this from nothing and I do not want a contract to take it away.

    Entity formation, partnership and operating agreements, contracts and leases, buying or selling a business, and disputes when they arise. Plain-language documents that you understand before you sign them.

  4. Probate and estate planning

    Someone has passed and nobody knows what happens to the house.

    Wills, trusts, powers of attorney and medical directives for the living. Probate, heirship and estate administration for the family left behind. Texas gives probate its own deadlines, so early advice saves cost and conflict.

  5. Personal injury

    The insurance company is calling me and I do not know what to say.

    Car and truck collisions, premises injuries and other negligence claims. We deal with the insurer so you do not have to, gather the medical record and pursue the compensation the facts support. Do not sign or record a statement before you have spoken with a lawyer.

  6. Tax matters

    The IRS letter says I owe more than I have.

    Responses to IRS and state notices, audits, payment arrangements and resolution of tax debt. Tax issues often sit beside an immigration or business file, and they are handled with that in view.

One problem usually touches another

A plea affects a visa. A divorce affects a petition. A business needs a will behind it. One office that sees the whole picture is the reason the firm practices this way.
